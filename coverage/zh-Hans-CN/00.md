# Chinese 00 — Sound, characters, and sentence foundations

`deck:zh-Hans-CN:00` contains exactly 200 text cards, `zh-00-0001`–`zh-00-0200`,
with one mapping each. All are Simplified production (`P`) in standard spoken
Chinese (Putonghua). [Chinese plan #3](https://github.com/25d-228/meiki-decks/issues/3#stage-00),
[execution #25](https://github.com/25d-228/meiki-decks/issues/25), and the
[pinned foundation guide](https://github.com/25d-228/meiki-decks/blob/e1d8239b7560f26b092cc7bb97c005738b34361f/languages/zh.md#61-foundation)
define this allocation. No prior Chinese, Spanish proficiency, Japanese character
knowledge or experience of another tonal variety is assumed.

A introduces identity/pronouns, naming, meaning and repetition questions, courtesy
formulas and relationships. B/C add everyday actions, people, objects and locations
as sound-bearing lexical units. D distinguishes actual readings and familiar
neutral words. E/F connect words and grammatical chunks to their actual tone and
phrase conditions. G consolidates useful functions rather than replaying frames
with changed names. Earlier exposure is cited when used; newly needed frame words
or bounded formulas are explicitly supported in the record and mapping. This does
not teach full aspect, complement, 把/被 or advanced clause systems early.

## Reading and reference decisions

`reading` covers only the complete hidden span, using lexical-tone Pinyin and
normal word spelling; neutral syllables are unmarked. It is not a full-sentence
transcript or a surface-tone field. Actual low third-tone, third-tone-sandhi and
一/不 changes are clearly labeled separately in support. This also distinguishes
neutral syllables that belong to the word, such as `bu` in 对不起, from the lexical
fourth tone of independent 不. Han words are typed in ordinary Simplified spelling;
no automatic script conversion, tone-number answers or Pinyin alternatives are added.

Primary references inform individual spelling/usage decisions, not copied cards:
