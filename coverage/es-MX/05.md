# Mexican Spanish 05 (C1) coverage matrix

## Purpose and sizing

This stage develops practical, educated contemporary central Mexican C1 communication through connected narration, explicit mood and evidential distinctions, stable reference, short-source synthesis, qualified argument, pragmatic interaction, and audience-sensitive expression. The planning size is 2,400 cards in sixty independent forty-card slices, `es-05-0001`–`es-05-2400`. The sixty distinct functional units below justify that size; numerical completion does not excuse a coverage gap or authorize filler. Completing this deck alone does not establish C1 certification or general proficiency.

## Immutable prerequisites and progression

Mexican Spanish 01–04 provide 5,200 accepted cards and unique normalized primary targets. Their cards, coverage, audio, and references remain unchanged, as does all Japanese, Korean, and French work. Stage 04 already provides practical tense/mood systems, counterfactuals, relatives, clitics, passives, reported speech, and B2 discourse. This stage develops their controlled discourse use and lexical precision; it does not repeat their paradigms.

Every slice requires Mexican Spanish 01–04 and all accepted earlier Stage 05 slices. A new same-slice or later-slice C1 construction may appear only as the explicitly introduced current target, never as incidental supporting language. Previously taught grammatical forms remain available as support; a new C1 communicative use must be taught as the current burden before being assumed. Higher discourse complexity belongs in the full cue, not in a long hidden answer.

## Binding card and originality rules

- Every card contains a complete natural sentence or short coherent exchange/passage, one literal cloze occurrence, and an answer exactly equal to that cloze.
- The cloze itself directly exercises the assigned production burden. A construction visible elsewhere does not justify an unrelated time, place, noun, name, number, document detail, or adjunct target.
- In finite clauses, normally hide only the assigned inflected form, necessary clitic plus form, required compound verb form, or genuinely indivisible reusable collocation. Keep subjects, objects, arbitrary complements/adverbs, productive triggers, factuality, and time relations visible.
- Do not hide whole or near-whole predicates/propositions except genuinely compact reusable fixed expressions. Preserve meaningful visible context; no long dictation answers, mechanical shortening, connector-plus-predicate fragments, or antecedent-plus-relative padding.
- Target boundaries stay within one sentence, quotation, paragraph, and speaker turn. Terminal punctuation remains outside the cloze and answer.
- Use NFC, standard accents and punctuation, natural central Mexican vocabulary and tense preferences, ustedes for plural address, and relationship-appropriate tú/usted. No productive vosotros/vos, regional imitation, caricature, IPA, phonetic respelling, or reading field.
- Vary syntax, scene, discourse purpose, speakers, and lexical burden. No tense/person/clitic/relative/preposition/gender/number/name/noun/formula substitution inventories; no dictionary blocks, copied text, template series, archaism, jargon, or inflated institutional prose.
- All primary targets and complete sentences are cumulatively unique under NFC, Unicode case-folding, and collapsed whitespace. Do not repair a duplicate by appending arbitrary context or modifying an earlier stage.
- Lemma and concise English meaning describe the actual target. Accepted alternatives must preserve the intended construction and contextual meaning. Omit unused optional fields; learner metadata contains no production, review, index, prerequisite, or validation language.
- Short-source tasks contain two or three short original statements in the cue. Preserve source identities and evidential limits; never assume an unseen source, fabricate real-institution findings, or hide a synthesized proposition.
- Health/access contexts concern communication and access only. Ordinary financial, legal, institutional, technology, and safety-related situations provide language practice, never professional advice or procedural guarantees.
- The accepted-target ledger below is synchronized with the cards and records the direct production burden for each accepted card. Empty planning ranges are not accepted evidence.

## Coverage distribution and review scope

The twelve focused reviews correspond to the twelve issue scope areas. Primary slice allocation is: narration/viewpoint S01–S05; mood/hypothesis S06–S11; modality/calibrated claims S12–S16; reference/information structure S17–S21; reporting/source attribution S22–S26; synthesis/explanation S27–S31; argument/counterargument S32–S36; stance/pragmatics S37–S41; interaction/mediation S42–S46; professional/academic communication S47–S51; public/cultural/personal domains S52–S56; lexical precision/delivery S57–S60. These areas are also recycled across domains.

After separate author/editor acceptance of every slice, run all twelve focused reviews and review every verified correction. Three contiguous audits cover S01–S20 (0001–0800), S21–S40 (0801–1600), and S41–S60 (1601–2400). Independent final audits A and B review the same complete final source and must each report the complete count and no remaining findings.

## Stage 06 boundary and phase boundary

Reserve sustained synthesis of long sources, highly opaque irony/allusion, dense literary rhetoric, unrestricted regional imitation, difficult historical forms, and specialist professional writing for Stage 06 or later. Future subjunctive and rare literary forms are not productive targets. No recognition-only archaic quotation is planned. Mild irony is limited and transparent.

This is Phase 1 source work only. No reference-file access, speech-model execution, audio generation, full archive build, sample preparation, Stage 06, or final bundle work is authorized. Existing mocked/tiny repository tests remain permitted.
