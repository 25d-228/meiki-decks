# Mexican Spanish 04 (B2) coverage matrix

## Stage purpose

Mexican Spanish 04 develops practical, educated B2 production and comprehension for contemporary central Mexican Spanish. It adds controlled multi-clause narration, hypothesis, reported speech, reference management, stance, negotiation, mediation, and source-based explanation across familiar personal, public, academic, service, and professional contexts. The stage contains exactly 2,000 cards: fifty independently authored and reviewed slices of 40 cards, from `es-04-0001` through `es-04-2000`.

## Binding stage-wide rules

- Each slice is a self-contained 40-card authoring and review unit. Its exact range is binding; no IDs may be added, removed, moved, or reused.
- Supporting language may come only from accepted Mexican Spanish 01–03 cards and accepted earlier Mexican Spanish 04 slices. Same-slice and later-slice targets are unavailable as support. A new construction may appear in its introducing slice only as the focused current target.
- Every card must contain one complete natural sentence or short coherent exchange, one `cloze`, and one focused production span. The declared construction—not merely its trigger or surrounding context—must be inside the cloze.
- For finite-clause targets, keep productive triggers, subjects, objects, complements, time references, and disambiguating context visible. Ordinarily hide only the assigned inflected form, a necessary clitic with that form, or an indivisible reusable chunk.
- Distribution counts below are coverage quotas, not paradigm plans. Vary speakers, verbs, syntax, lexical domains, discourse roles, and communicative purposes. Do not generate person, tense, mood, clitic, passive, relative, noun, connector, or document-field substitution series.
- Use cohesive multi-clause sentences and short exchanges. Do not use connector dictionaries, connector-plus-predicate targets, trigger inventories, conjugation tables, synonym lists, arbitrary antecedent padding, unsupported essays, translated textbook prose, specialist documents, or process scripts.
- All normalized primary targets and complete sentences must be unique across Mexican Spanish 01–04. Do not repair duplication by enlarging a cloze with arbitrary context.
- Follow educated contemporary central Mexican usage. Use `ustedes` for all plural address; never productively use `vosotros` or `vos`. Keep `tú`, `usted`, or `ustedes`, register, and relationship stable within an interaction.
- Preserve ordinary Mexican distribution of the simple preterite for completed periods and the present perfect for explicit current relevance. Avoid artificial preference for compound past forms.
- Registers are ordinary conversation, courteous service, professional speech, neutral public information, and neutral written language. Avoid unexplained slang, caricature, archaism, and mixtures modeled on another regional variety.
- Recycle stable vowels, Mexican phrase rhythm, lexical and grammatical stress, tap/trill, Mexican `ll/y`, `j`, `ñ`, consonant linking, connected clitic groups, and clause grouping. Use no IPA or phonetic respelling.
- Use canonical NFC spelling, standard accents and capitalization, inverted question and exclamation marks, appropriate quotation marks, and natural punctuation. Terminal punctuation remains outside the cloze.
- Relative, clitic, passive, and discourse targets must have clear antecedents and stable reference. Never manufacture answer uniqueness with an appended name, noun, date, number, place, modifier, or adverb.
- Conditions and mood contrasts require explicit time relations or contextual contrasts. Productive triggers remain outside the cloze while the assigned mood or tense burden remains inside it.
- Source use is limited to short, transparent reports, observations, interviews, talks, reviews, articles, notices, and public information represented within the card. No copied passages or dependence on inaccessible sources is allowed.
- Health content covers communication, access, scheduling, and non-diagnostic description only. Financial, institutional, legal, medical, and safety contexts provide ordinary language practice, never professional advice.
- Reserve sustained synthesis of several long sources, highly implicit stance, irony, allusion, rhetorical analysis, extended academic argument, advanced stylistic switching, future subjunctive, rare literary tenses, highly formal relative chains, difficult discourse-level clitic reference, specialized terminology, and broad regional-variety production for Spanish 05 or later.
- This matrix is Phase 1 source planning only. It authorizes no voice-reference access, audio generation, denoising, archive work, listening samples, application interaction, or Spanish 05 work.

## Slice 01 — Completed events and background

- **IDs:** `es-04-0001`–`es-04-0040` (40 cards).

- **Register and relationship:** Neutral conversation among acquaintances and colleagues; consistent `tú` or third-person narration.
- **Listening and production targets:** Recognize event/background contrasts in two- or three-clause accounts; produce one fairly scoped past form from visible chronology and aspect cues.

- **Exclusions:** No present perfect or past perfect target burden, dense timeline shifts, literary narration, or tense-substitution series.
