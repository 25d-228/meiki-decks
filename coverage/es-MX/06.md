# Mexican Spanish 06 (C2 and advanced-use bridge) coverage matrix

## Purpose and coverage-led sizing

This stage develops distinct advanced-use tasks in educated contemporary central Mexican Spanish. The initial plan contains 2,605 cards in 67 independently authored and reviewed slices: 40 cards in each slice except S56–S60, which each contain 25 cards to keep regional recognition limited. These are proposed coverage ranges, not a requirement to preserve overlapping unaccepted candidates. Reviews may retire redundant candidates and synchronize subsequent ranges before acceptance. Numerical completion cannot replace coverage or justify filler. This audio-cloze bridge does not certify C2 proficiency.

## Immutable prerequisites and progression

Mexican Spanish 01–05 provide 7,462 accepted cards. Stage 05 already teaches controlled viewpoint, mood, evidence limits, short-source synthesis, qualified argument, reference, mediation, and register adaptation. Stage 06 advances through interacting scopes, nested attribution, unequal knowledge, consequential reservations, selective revision, and controlled transformations—not harder synonyms, longer passages, or new labels alone. Each slice below states the required advance. The accepted ledger records the actual target and direct learning burden of each accepted card.

All earlier Spanish and all Japanese, Korean, and French work remain unchanged. Each slice requires Mexican Spanish 01–05 and accepted earlier Stage 06 slices only. Previously learned grammar may support a new discourse task. A new same-slice or later-slice construction or communicative use may appear only as the explicitly introduced current target; it cannot be assumed as incidental support.

## Binding card, source, and originality rules

- Use a complete natural sentence or short coherent passage/exchange. Put all evidence needed for interpretation in the cue; no unseen source or unexplained cultural knowledge.
- Every literal target occurs exactly once and equals the answer. The target itself realizes the assigned burden; a construction elsewhere in the cue does not justify an unrelated detail target.
- For a finite clause, ordinarily hide only the assigned inflected form, necessary clitic plus form, required compound form, or genuinely indivisible reusable collocation. Keep triggers, licensing meaning, factuality, subjects, objects, complements, adverbs, and time relations visible.
- Do not hide complete or near-complete propositions, except compact reusable fixed expressions. Keep useful visible context. No connector-plus-predicate fragments, mechanical shortening, relative padding, or arbitrary enlargement for uniqueness.
- Keep the target within one sentence, quotation, paragraph, and speaker turn; terminal punctuation stays outside it.
- Primary targets and complete cues are unique across Spanish 01–06 under NFC, Unicode case-folding, and collapsed whitespace. Distinct strings alone do not prove distinct tasks. Do not repair duplication by altering earlier stages or appending arbitrary names, nouns, dates, places, numbers, modifiers, or complements.
- Vary syntax, domains, relationships, evidence patterns, and communicative acts. No dictionary, idiom catalog, tense/number/gender/relative/connector table, document-field inventory, or template substitution block.
- Use contemporary central Mexican grammar, vocabulary, tense preferences, and stable tú/usted relationships; plural address uses ustedes. Standard accents, inverted punctuation, and canonical NFC spelling are required.
- Non-Mexican forms are permitted only as explicitly framed, bounded recognition sources, never productive targets or a second voice. Do not generalize an individual's pragmatic intention into a universal regional fact.
- Lemma, concise English meaning, and genuine accepted alternatives describe the actual span. Omit unused optional fields, reading, IPA, respelling, and internal process language.
- Original source statements may disagree. Preserve agency, qualification, factuality, and evidence status. Do not fabricate real-person or real-institution findings, quotations, or authority.
- Health/access situations are communication and access tasks only; no diagnosis, treatment, legal, financial, technical-safety, or other professional advice.

## Advanced-use and phase boundaries

No productive future subjunctive, rare historical forms, artificial archaism, opaque allusion, unrestricted regional imitation, specialist writing, long unseen-source examination, or pipeline changes. No recognition-only historical quotation is planned. Complexity must remain usable as a self-contained short cue under the existing runner; do not lengthen cues to manufacture progression.

Phase 1 is source-only. Reference access, speech models, audio, full archives, samples, Phase 2, another stage, final-bundle work, and publication are excluded. Existing mocked/tiny repository tests remain permitted.
