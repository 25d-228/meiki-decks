# Korean 06 coverage matrix

## Stage outcome and completion rule

Korean 06 is the final advanced stage for contemporary standard South Korean. It bridges TOPIK 6-oriented comprehension to advanced functional production, register control, interaction, editing, mediation, and source-based communication. It makes no near-native claim.

The accepted stage contains exactly 3,200 cards in 80 cumulative generation slices of 40 cards:

- IDs: `ko-06-0001`–`ko-06-3200`.
- Immutable prerequisite source: Korean 00–05, exactly 9,780 accepted cards.
- Completed Korean 00–06 total: exactly 12,980 accepted cards.
- The 80 slices have disjoint primary burdens but form one cumulative acceptance chain.
- Exactly 3,200 cards is valid only if every listed burden is evidenced without mechanical variants, prerequisite duplication, rarity, or filler.

## Card, language, and source contract

Every card must use one original, natural complete Korean sentence or short coherent passage as its audio cue and test one focused production span. Visible context must make the answer fair. The cloze must remain within one sentence, quotation, paragraph, and speaker turn. Terminal punctuation stays outside `cloze` and `answer`; `answer` equals `cloze` exactly. Input is strict Hangul with current standard South Korean spelling and spacing. Romanization and `reading` are prohibited.

Only independently genuine contextual alternatives may appear in `accepted_answers`. English `meaning` support is concise. Optional `grammar`, `register`, and `note` fields appear only when they directly help the learner; unused fields are omitted. Internal generation, slice, review, audit, validation, index, and cloze-design language is prohibited. Every card declares `work/audio/ko-KR/06/<card-id>.mp3`.

Whole-sentence clozes are limited to compact useful fixed expressions. Long dictation, arbitrary enlarged clozes, mechanical name or number substitutions, and lightly altered Korean 00–05 material are prohibited.

## Binding per-slice position contract

Every slice binds its ID positions as follows:

- First 16 IDs: `C`, construction, ending, collocation, dense-written-form, or other linguistic-form production.
- Next 12 IDs: `F`, communicative-function or discourse-operation production.
- Next 4 IDs: `R`, relationship/register production. `R` specifically tests selection or reformulation of language for a declared audience, relationship, hierarchy, speech level, or genre; it is not a context-only label.
- Next 4 IDs: `L`, production whose fairness also depends on recovering standard connected delivery, grouping, source ownership, turn force, reduction, or sentence-final intonation.
- Last 4 IDs: `O`, production focused on standard spelling, spacing, punctuation, quotation, number notation, or phrase grouping.

Thus every row contains `C16/F12/R4/L4/O4`. All cards remain production cards and all cues receive listening review. Every slice includes spoken and written production, at least one spontaneous reformulation, and at least one relationship-sensitive choice. A card counts in exactly one primary position.

## Binding global inventory

The accepted cards must collectively distinguish advanced final stance; observation, report, inference, appearance, recollection, assumption, probability, and suspended judgment; qualification by population, time, location, condition, purpose, exception, degree, and evidential reach; concession, limitation, contrast, counterexpectation, hypothesis, counterfactuality, inevitability, constraint, threshold, cause, condition, correlation, purpose, means, consequence, comparison, substitution, temporal/aspectual control, embedded discourse, nominalization, passive and causative responsibility, cohesion, recoverable ellipsis, rhetorical organization, editing, and speech-level consistency.

Productive constructions must be contemporary and contextual. Useful forms such as `-에 불과하다`, `-에 지나지 않다`, `-기는커녕`, `-을 뿐더러`, `-는 한`, `-지 않는 한`, `-다고 치다`, `-는 셈 치다`, `-을 경우`, `-았더라면`, `-았을 텐데`, `-았어야 했다`, `-을 수밖에 없다`, `-지 않을 수 없다`, `-기 마련이다`, `-게 되어 있다`, `-을 정도로`, `-다 못해`, and `-기에 이르다` are admitted only where natural. Marginal or archaizing forms such as `-다손 치더라도` are excluded from production.

Functions include precise qualification; evidence evaluation; calibrated certainty; concession; causal and conditional reasoning; indirect disagreement, criticism, correction, requests, refusals, deferral, conditional acceptance, and negotiation; authority, delegation, escalation, accountability, and handoff; turn management, interruption, repair, backchannels, and topic shifts; emotionally appropriate speech; recoverable humor, irony, understatement, implication, and ambiguity; speeches, interviews, debates, meetings, presentations, reports, proposals, editorials, and analysis; faithful quotation, paraphrase, comparison, synthesis, mediation, editing, and spontaneous professional explanation.

Domains include workplace and organizations; education and research; public services and institutions; media and information; economy and everyday finance; technology; civic and social issues; environment, safety, and infrastructure; health and welfare communication; culture and arts; projects and negotiation; and source-based rhetorical communication. Specialist terms require general-use context. Binding Sino-Korean families include `시행/실시/수행/실행`, `검토/검증/확인/점검`, `근거/자료/사례/증거`, `결과/성과/효과/영향`, `비용/경비/지출/부담`, `감소/절감/축소/완화`, `보도/발표/공개/전달`, `협의/논의/조정/합의`, `책임/권한/소관/재량`, `추정/추측/전망/예측`, and `수정/정정/개정/보완` in natural collocations. Isolated Hanja is excluded.

Productive registers are formal spoken `합니다체`, ordinary polite `해요체`, intimate `해체` with an explicit stable relationship, neutral written `한다체`, formal institutional writing, and accessible contemporary literary or rhetorical prose. Honorification, titles, `께/께서`, `드리다`, `모시다`, `여쭙다`, `뵙다`, and lexical honorifics must match the represented relationship. Power, age, expertise, institutional role, familiarity, and setting remain distinct. Register switching requires a changed audience, quoted voice, deliberate distance, intimacy shift, or public/private boundary.

Generational, occupational, institutional, regional, and social variation is recognition-only. The learner must produce a contemporary standard South Korean paraphrase or response. A nonstandard form cannot be a primary target or accepted answer.

Pragmatic force must be recoverable from role, prior action, timing, consequence, wording, or response. Intonation may support but cannot supply the only evidence. The source distinguishes questions from criticism, suggestions from orders, refusal from deferral, conditional acceptance from reluctant agreement, concern from warning, understatement from literal minimization, irony from misinformation, humor from ridicule, and controlled ambiguity from missing context.

Orthography covers bound nouns, auxiliaries, counters, dependent constructions, names and titles, institutional compounds, standard contractions, `되/돼`, `안/않`, `데/대`, `든/던`, `로서/로써`, `맞히다/맞추다`, quotation, nested quotation, natural Korean commas and colons, parentheses, ellipses and dashes, Korean and Arabic numbers, dates, times, percentages, units, ranges, approximations, statistics, lists, and paragraph punctuation. Semicolons are used only where natural Korean prose warrants them.

Listening covers standard pronunciation cues, phrase rhythm, lawful connected delivery and reductions, final intonation, source ownership, quotation boundaries, qualifications, enumerations, numbers, contrast, rapid group interaction, separate-turn interruption and repair, controlled emotion, presentation cadence, interview follow-up, debate challenge, and spontaneous explanation. A cloze never crosses speakers, and simulated overlap cannot obscure the target.

## Normalization, uniqueness, and originality

Primary normalization is deterministic: Unicode NFC, compatibility-width normalization, leading/trailing whitespace removal, and collapse of internal whitespace runs to one space. Meaningful internal Korean punctuation is retained. Terminal punctuation is absent because it remains outside the target.

Every normalized primary target must be unique across Korean 00–06. Punctuation-light, spacing-light, morphology-aware, sentence-frame, and semantic diagnostics identify cosmetic or mechanical near-duplicates for editorial review; diagnostic similarity does not itself prohibit a genuinely distinct advanced burden or authorize nonstandard spelling.

An accepted answer must be independently natural in the same visible context and cannot hide a duplicate, relax standard spelling, change relationship or register, or enlarge the cloze arbitrarily. A duplicate is repaired by selecting a genuinely different focused burden and rewriting the sentence naturally. Originality checks compare Korean 00–05, accepted Korean 06, and the current slice. Copied sentences, distinctive passages, living-author imitation, mechanical lexical series, repeated sentence frames, and context-noun-only variants are rejected.

## Global exclusions

Exclude rare or archaic grammar, isolated Hanja, unexplained classical quotations, obscure proverbs, specialist jargon without general-use context, dialect imitation as production, stigmatizing sociolect caricature, mechanical ending conversion, name/value substitution, synonym lists without collocations, trivia, long dictation, near-native claims, and private-culture trick answers. Also exclude financial, legal, or medical directives; partisan persuasion; gratuitous violence; harassment; humiliation-based humor; prescriptive claims that one style is universally neutral; and any nonstandard recognition form as a primary or accepted answer.
